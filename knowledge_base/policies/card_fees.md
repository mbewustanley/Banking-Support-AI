# Fictional Banking Policy: Card Fees

Policy ID: CARD-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Synthetic development example

## Foreign Transaction Fees

A foreign transaction fee may apply when a card is used to make a
purchase in a foreign currency. The applicable fee depends on the
account terms associated with the customer's card.

Customers should consult their account terms or contact support to
confirm the fee applicable to their account.

## Duplicate Card Charges

If a customer believes a card transaction has been charged twice,
they should review the transaction history and contact customer
support with the transaction dates and amounts.

A duplicate charge must be investigated before a refund is confirmed.

## Fee Refunds

A fee refund is not automatic. Support must review the fee and the
applicable account terms before confirming eligibility.
